About this mode and its limits
WHAT YOU ARE LEARNING

Real games, not memorized responses

Black can choose moves outside the seven lessons. Stockfish calculates locally in your browser and now reviews evaluation swings after every White move. Legal moves, checks, castling, promotion and game results are handled by chess.js.

No paid subscription or account required. Initial engine download needs internet.
